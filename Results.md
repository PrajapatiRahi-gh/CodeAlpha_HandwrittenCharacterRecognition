## Results

The Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model was developed to recognize handwritten digits using the MNIST dataset. The model's performance was examined using accuracy and loss graphs, a confusion matrix, and sample handwritten digit images.

### Results Analysis

* **Model Accuracy:** The accuracy graph shows that training and validation accuracy increase during the training process.
* **Model Loss:** The loss graph shows that training and validation loss decrease as the model learns.
* **Confusion Matrix:** The confusion matrix shows that most predictions are concentrated along the diagonal, indicating that the model correctly classifies many handwritten digits.
* **Sample Digits:** The sample images demonstrate the handwritten digits used to examine the model's recognition results.
